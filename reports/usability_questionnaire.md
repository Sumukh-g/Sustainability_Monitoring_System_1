# Dashboard Usability Evaluation Framework (Not Yet Administered)

No participant data or results are claimed. With ethics approval and informed consent, ask representative managers/engineers to complete defined tasks (find current PUE, identify a critical anomaly, interpret a forecast, locate its evidence-linked action, export a period) and record completion, errors and time.

Rate 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree): navigation is clear; KPI units are clear; charts are readable; anomaly severity is understandable; forecasts distinguish observed and predicted values; recommendations explain their trigger; the system supports intervention identification; response time is acceptable; disclaimers are clear. Collect optional qualitative comments. Report recruitment, sampling, protocol, descriptive results and limitations without inferring beyond the sample.
